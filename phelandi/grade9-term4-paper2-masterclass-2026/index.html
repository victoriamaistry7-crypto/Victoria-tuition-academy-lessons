The three measurement families

Perimeter is the distance around a flat shape. Units: cm, m, km.

Area is the space inside a flat shape. Units: cm2, m2.

Surface area is the total area of the outside of a 3D object. Units: cm2.

Volume is the space occupied. Units: cm3, m3.

Mini formula wall

A=12bh   (triangle)
C=2πr   (circle circumference)
V=Abase×L   (prism)
V=πr2h   (cylinder)

Area & perimeter

WORKED EXAMPLE / RECTANGLE

Different units, different answers

A=l×w

Area: 8 × 5 = 40 cm2

P=2(l+w)

Perimeter: 2(8 + 5) = 26 cm

WORKED EXAMPLE / TRIANGLE

One-half of a rectangle

A=12×b×h

Substitute: 12×12×5=30

Answer: 30 cm2

Height must be perpendicular to the base, not necessarily a sloping side.

WORKED EXAMPLE / CIRCLE

Radius or diameter?

A=πr2

3.14 × 72 = 153.86 cm2

C=2πr

2 × 3.14 × 7 = 43.96 cm

COMPOSITE / IRREGULAR SHAPE

Subtract the missing rectangle

Important: The pink cut-out diagram above is schematic. The marked dimensions define the measurements.

Method
  1. Calculate the full rectangle: 10 × 8 = 80 cm2.
  2. Calculate the missing corner: 4 × 3 = 12 cm2.
  3. Subtract: 80 − 12 = 68 cm2.
FORMULAE & CONVERSIONS

Write these before the exam

Parallelogram: A=bh
Trapezium: A=12(a+b)h
Circle: C=πd
s=81=9 cm   (square root example)

Unit traps: 1 cm = 10 mm, but 1 cm2 = 100 mm2. Also 1 m2 = 10 000 cm2.

If a question asks for perimeter, add external side lengths; if it asks for area, calculate square units.

03

Surface area & volume

00:45–01:20 • Cubes, rectangular/triangular prisms and cylinders

RECTANGULAR PRISM

Count all six faces

V=lwh

8 × 4 × 3 = 96 cm3

SA=2(lw+lh+wh)

2(32 + 24 + 12) = 136 cm2

RIGHT TRIANGULAR PRISM

Find the triangular base first

V=(12×3×4)×10

6 × 10 = 60 cm3.

SA=2B+pL

2(6) + (3 + 4 + 5) × 10 = 132 cm2.

CYLINDER

Two circles and a curved side

V=πr2h

3.14 × 32 × 10 = 282.60 cm3

SA=2πr2+2πrh

2 × 3.14 × 9 + 2 × 3.14 × 3 × 10 = 244.92 cm2

UNITS & CAPACITY

Don't lose easy conversion marks

1 cm3 = 1 mL
1 000 cm3 = 1 L
1 m3 = 1 000 000 cm3 = 1 000 L
Example: A box holds 2 500 cm3.
2 500 ÷ 1 000 = 2.5 L.
SURFACE AREA CHECKLIST

SA vs V

For volume: Calculate cross-sectional area and multiply by prism length, or use the cylinder formula.

For surface area: Add every exposed outside face. On a triangular prism, that means 2 triangles + 3 rectangles.

Cylinder: 2 circular bases + curved surface area.

No top / open container? Don't count a lid that isn't there. The question must specify this.

TRANSFORMATION RULES

Learn the rule, then apply it

x-axis reflection: (x,y)→(x,−y)
y-axis reflection: (x,y)→(−x,y)
line y = x: (x,y)→(y,x)
Translate by (a,b): (x,y)→(x+a,y+b)
Scale factor k at origin: (x,y)→(kx,ky)
Example: Reflect P(−2, 4) in y = x.
Swap the coordinates, giving P′(4, −2).
Enlargement: Lengths and perimeter multiply by k; area multiplies by k2. For k = 3, area becomes 9 times bigger.
