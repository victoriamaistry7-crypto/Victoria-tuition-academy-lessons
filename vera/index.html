How evidence labels work

Version 0.1
Strong

Supported by clinical guidelines, approvals or multiple good-quality studies for the specific use.

Moderate

Promising evidence, but the effect, population or certainty is more limited.

Limited

Plausible or popular, but not enough reliable evidence for a confident beauty claim.

Caution

May be irritating, inappropriate for some users, prescription-only, or needs clinical assessment.

Red flags beat beauty goals.

If you have rapid or patchy hair loss, severe swelling, eye pain/vision changes, spreading infection, a changing/bleeding mole, or significant new unwanted hair with menstrual changes, seek appropriate medical care rather than experimenting with more products.
