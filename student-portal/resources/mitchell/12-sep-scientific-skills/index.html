01 · Scientific Method

Know what changes.

The key pattern is simple: I change → I measure → I keep the rest the same.

INDEPENDENT VARIABLE
I CHANGE

The factor you deliberately change.

→
DEPENDENT VARIABLE
I MEASURE

The result you observe or record.

→
CONTROLLED VARIABLES
I LOCK

Factors kept constant for a fair test.

Choose the role

Investigation: “How does the length of a resistance wire affect the electric current?”

IV
Length of wire

What is deliberately changed?

DV
Electric current

What result is measured?

CV
Battery voltage

What should stay constant?

BUILD A HYPOTHESIS
If the length of the wire increases, then the electric current will decrease.

Use: If [IV] changes, then [DV] will [change].

04 · Biological diagrams

Draw like a scientist.

A scientific diagram should be clear, accurate and easy to mark.

The 4-rule checklist

01
Double boundary
Cell wall outside + cell membrane inside.
02
No shading
Use clean, continuous lines.
03
Straight labels
Horizontal ruler lines that do not cross.
04
Title
Clear, underlined scientific title.
Spot the mistake →

A learner shades the whole cell to make it “realistic”.

Why it is a problem:
Scientific drawings should make structures easy to see using clear line work, not artistic shading.
