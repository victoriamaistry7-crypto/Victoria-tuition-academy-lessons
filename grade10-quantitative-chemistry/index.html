Part 1 • Foundation

What is a mole?

A mole is a counting unit. Just as one dozen means 12 objects, one mole means a fixed number of particles.

1 dozen

12 objects

Example: 12 eggs.

1 mole

6.022 × 10²³ particles

This number is Avogadro’s constant.

NA = 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹

The word “particle” depends on the substance: atoms, molecules or formula units.

Part 2 • Molar mass

Turn a chemical formula into g·mol⁻¹

Molar mass is the mass of one mole of a substance. Add the atomic masses of every atom in the formula.

Worked example: H₂O

H = 1 and O = 16

2(1) + 16 = 18 g·mol⁻¹
