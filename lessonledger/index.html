Frequently asked questions

Does it work without Wi‑Fi?

Yes. The downloadable dashboard runs locally in a browser. You need an internet connection to complete a purchase and download the files.

Does it sync across my phone and laptop?

No automatic cloud sync. You can export a JSON backup and restore it on another device. Avoid using shared devices for student records.

Can it collect customer payments automatically?

No. You record payments manually after receipt. The statement feature is an administrative summary, not certified accounting software.

What do I receive?

One offline HTML app, one Excel tracker, a PDF user guide and two copy-ready tutoring templates.
